Biography

Dohyun Kim has been serving as an Assistant Professor in the Computer Engineering Major, Division of Artificial Intelligence Engineering at Korea Maritime & Ocean University (KMOU) since March 2025. He received his Ph.D. in Information Security with a specialization in Digital Forensics from Korea University in August 2019, under the supervision of Prof. Sangjin Lee. Prior to his current role, he was an Assistant Professor at Catholic University of Pusan (Apr 2020 – Feb 2025), a Research Professor at Korea University (Sep 2019 – Mar 2020), and a Researcher at ETRI (Jul 2017 – Aug 2019). His research interests span Digital Forensics (PC, mobile, memory, drone, and IoT), Vulnerability Research and Reverse Engineering (firmware, malware, and obfuscation/packer analysis), Maritime Cybersecurity, and AI-driven Security integrating NLP and large language models (LLMs).
